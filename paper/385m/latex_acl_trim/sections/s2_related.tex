\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
% Adapted from the earlier related-work review; only works in the bib files are cited.
\textbf{Translation for African languages.} Community benchmarks and corpora made progress measurable: Masakhane \citep{nekoto2020participatory}, MAFAND-MT \citep{adelani2022mafand}, FLORES-200 and its community successor FLORES+ \citep{goyal2022flores101,oldi2025floresplus}, NTREX-128 \citep{federmann2022ntrex}, SALT \citep{akera2022salt} and SMOL \citep{caswell2025smol}. NLLB-200 \citep{nllb2022nllb} is the reference system. Recent language models for African languages are much larger than ours \citep{yu2026afriquellm,akera2025sunflower}; TranslatePsy-AfriSLM \citep{gritta2026translatepsy} is the nearest small competitor. For science text, AfriScience-MT \citep{abdulmumin2026afriscience} uses SSA-COMET \citep{li2025ssacomet} as its main metric.

\textbf{Reinforcement learning for translation.} GRPO \citep{shao2024deepseekmath,yu2025dapo} powers recent translation systems with reasoning-style training \citep{feng2025mtr1zero,wang2025deeptrans,cheng2025seedx,zheng2025hunyuanmt}, and TranslateGemma rewards with an ensemble of quality models \citep{finkelstein2026translategemma}. Closest to us, other work trains NLLB models with round-trip or reference-free rewards \citep{attia2026roundtrip,garciaestrada2026reffree} and studies reward hacks in quality-estimation rewards \citep{liu2026walar,han2026milmmtrl}. Whether RL generalises beyond what supervised training memorises has been asked for reasoning \citep{chu2025sft,yue2025doesrl}. For translation, we searched for a comparison at matched computing time and found none; the nearest compares reference-free RL with three epochs of supervised training \citep{garciaestrada2026reffree}.

\textbf{Decoding and evaluation.} Minimum Bayes risk and quality-aware decoding \citep{freitag2022mbr,fernandes2022qualityaware} aim at similar gains without training, so we measure them as controls. AfriCOMET \citep{wang2024africomet}, chrF++ and sacreBLEU \citep{popovic2017chrfpp,post2018call} and the paired bootstrap \citep{koehn2004significance} are our metrics and tests. Learned rewards are easy to over-optimise \citep{gao2023scaling,coste2024ensembles}, which is why we keep the metrics we report separate from the metrics we reward. We claim no priority for RL in translation, for small models, or for language coverage.
