\textbf{Automatic scoring only.} The references of FLORES+, NTREX-128 and MAFAND-MT are human translations, but all scoring is automatic: no human judgement of system outputs was collected, the neural metrics are of one family and AfriCOMET has a length and entity bias.
\textbf{One training seed} per model and stage. Seed spread was measured only for the stage-A recipe at 100 steps on 20 languages (one pair of extra seeds, 0.06 \chrfpp{} \ci{-0.02}{+0.14}); the mixed step and per-language differences under about 0.5 \chrfpp{} cannot be separated from seed noise, and a second SFT seed is impractical (stage B alone is 44.8 \GPUh{}).
\textbf{The matched-compute control is one run}: one seed, one learning rate, one data mix with NLLB-3.3B targets and one evaluated budget point; the decode-time guard's best setting was at the edge of its grid and stronger penalties were explored on dev only.
\textbf{Reward-family overlap.} Finished, loop and language-ID rates are optimised by construction, SSA-COMET-QE is of the reward family, \chrfpp{} is partly optimised for 5 + 7 languages, and the independent loop detector shares the repetition concept and is blind to non-repetitive failure.
\textbf{Ablations are limited}: three arms, 100 steps, one run each, 20 languages; later stages and the other reward components were not ablated, and mid-run changes (Ethical considerations) mean the stages are not one clean design.
\textbf{Engine and decoding.} Headline numbers are vLLM 0.32.0 bf16 greedy; the bf16 engine-parity gate was not met (fp32 agrees), the RL model was not run in fp32, and beam and MBR cover 20 languages $\times$ 200 sentences and one beam width.
\textbf{Coverage and comparisons.} 51 of 125 languages have no reference test; the model fails on Arabic dialects, Tamazight, Dinka, Tamasheq and Kanuri, where RL increases English output; comparisons with the earlier 270M model and with NLLB are composites (data, steps, reward; FLORES-200 against FLORES+; beam against greedy); document results rest on 28 papers per language and whole-document decoding remains unusable.
\textbf{Not run.} Human judgement of outputs; the NLLB-data ablation; packed-shard contamination scans; the 54-language decoding run; a science SFT at alpha 1.0 followed by RL, a second seed of E-14 and a long-document RL from the E-14 model.
