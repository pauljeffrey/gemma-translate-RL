## 0. Conventions

**Status.** `supported` = the number is in a report that states how it was computed, the comparison is controlled enough for the claim as worded, and the caveat column does not remove it. `weak` = the number is real but the claim as one would like to word it is not supported (confound, no control, single comparison, reward-family overlap, descriptive only). `pending` = the experiment has not finished or has not been run; no number may be written.
