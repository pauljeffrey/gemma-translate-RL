\section{Evaluation Protocol}\label{sec:eval}
% [M11 CT1-CT6 X1 X2 X5 X7 X9]
\textbf{Benchmarks.} English to X on FLORES+ devtest (1,012 sentences; 54 languages), NTREX-128 (24), MAFAND-MT (15), SmolSent \citep{caswell2025smol}, SALT \citep{akera2022salt}, GlotStoryBook \citep{kargaran2023glotlid}, IbomMT \citep{kalejaiye2025ibom}, FLORES-MOZ \citep{moznlp2026floresmoz} and AfriScience-MT (six languages). The headline set is the \textbf{93 benchmark-language cells} FLORES+ (54), NTREX (24), MAFAND-MT (15).

\textbf{Metrics.} \textbf{chrF++} is chrF with word 1--2-grams (sacreBLEU \texttt{word\_order=2}); \textbf{plain chrF} uses character n-grams only (word order 0); spBLEU uses the \texttt{flores200} tokenizer. Held-out AfriCOMET is the reference-based \texttt{africomet-stl-1.1} (40 FLORES+ languages). \emph{Finished} and \emph{loop} rates are the reward's own guards. Macros weight languages equally. sacreBLEU 2.6.0 (from the install log of the evaluation machine): chrF++ is \texttt{CHRF(word\_order=2)} with the defaults (character n-grams up to 6, $\beta=2$, no lowercasing) after NFC normalisation of hypotheses and references, and spBLEU is BLEU with \texttt{tokenize=flores200}; the full signature strings were not recorded.

\textbf{Decoding and intervals.} Greedy, up to 768 new tokens, vLLM 0.32.0 bf16 on an A100. Intervals are 95\% paired bootstrap over test sentences (1,000 resamples; Poisson weights per source sentence shared across parallel languages for RL-versus-SFT claims); they cover sentence variance only, not training seeds. One training seed per model unless stated. A \textbf{decode-time loop guard} is a vLLM frequency penalty during greedy decoding, chosen on the SFT dev grid (720 sentences, 18 languages) by a rule fixed in advance.

\textbf{Independence rule.} Metrics inside the reward are not evidence of RL gains: the two QE models, consensus and pseudo-reference chrF++ terms, and the finished, loop, copy and language-ID guards. SSA-COMET-QE (the AfriScience headline) is of the reward members' family and is shown only beside chrF and AfriCOMET. Evidence is limited to chrF++, chrF and spBLEU against human references and the held-out reference-based AfriCOMET (same family as the QE member; never a reward). An independent repetition detector and the NLLB-218 language-ID model (evaluation only) check the reward's loop and language guards (full table: Appendix~\ref{app:indep}).
