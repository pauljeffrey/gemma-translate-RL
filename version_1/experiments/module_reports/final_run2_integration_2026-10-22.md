## 8. Wind-down / lr_scale rule if the paper-level finished rate is flat at step 150-200

Monitor `bench_aux/afrisci_dev_doc/finished_rate` (step 0: **0.927** for the A2W start, not 0.773, which was the final_1433 start; evaluated every 50 steps), `bench_aux/afrisci_dev_doc/chrf_macro` (0.927 / 44.02 at step 0), `bench/chrf_macro` (48.79), general dev (41.97), weak dev (24.90). F0, P0, S0 = the step-0 values. At step 150 and 200:
1. **Flat objective**: finished_rate - F0 < +0.02 at BOTH steps and paper chrF++ - P0 < +0.5 and sentence chrF++ - S0 < +0.3 -> the long-document objective is not moving; `winddown 50` at step 200 (ends at ~250, the best checkpoint is already pushed) and free the GPU for a re-tune (greedy scale 0.5 / lr 1.5e-6), because the remaining 450 steps at the same settings are unlikely to differ.
2. **Flat but improving elsewhere** (finished_rate flat, sentence chrF++ - S0 >= +0.3 or paper chrF++ - P0 >= +0.5): continue (the finished rate is already 0.93, little headroom; judge by chrF++).
3. **Regression**: finished_rate < F0 - 0.04 at two consecutive evaluations (or paper chrF++ < P0 - 1.5) -> `lr_scale 0.5` at once; if the next evaluation (50 steps) is not back within F0 - 0.02 -> `winddown 50`.
4. The general stability criteria of the first report (KL > 0.10 for 10 steps -> `lr_scale 0.5`, forgetting thresholds relative to 41.97 / 24.90, `min_share` > 0.30, `reward/fail/stuffed_output` > 0.05 -> look at the func/pad terms or set `anchors_stuff_gate: 0`) stay.
