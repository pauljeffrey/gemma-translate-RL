0. FRAMING (read first)
   * Step 0 (reports/ge_ez_step0_2026-10-25.md) showed that our tokenizer needs 1.31x (amh), 1.53x (tir), 1.47x (fon) NLLB's tokens, close to the 1.39x mean
     over eight African languages; tokenisation therefore does NOT explain the amh gap to NLLB (19.0 vs 38.0 chrF++) by itself.
   * The swap is therefore justified by sequence length / decoding speed / context budget (tokens per sentence amh -22%, tir -35%, fon -27% at N=3,000 in
     this build) and is tested as a SEPARATE FACTOR; it is NOT presented as a proven quality fix. Capacity (270M vs 1B) is a different factor.
   * The tokenizer swap is an experimental factor on its own. The swapped tokenizer is bit-identical to the original on all text without Ethiopic
     (Ge'ez variants) / Fon-specific characters (Fon variants) -- measured, see the build report. The Fon variant is NOT "safe": it also changes the
     tokenisation of Ewe, Bambara and other languages that use the same characters (measured numbers in the build report); those languages must be in the
     evaluation and the training mixture of every Fon arm.

   D4 "Fon swap"                          : adopted only if F3 passes the same tests on fon AND Ewe/Bambara/Lingala/Wolof/Hausa/Yoruba show no loss beyond

   D5 "Capacity vs tokenizer"             : tokenizer effect = A5g - A0 at 270M and A3g - A3 at 1B; capacity effect = A3 - A0 and A3g - A5g. Claims about capacity

6. REPRODUCTION / ARTEFACT INTEGRITY
   Every checkpoint used must have the sha256 listed in reports/data/tokswap_2026-10-25/SHA256SUMS.txt; the tokenizer files used for scoring are the ones in the
   same directory; the evaluation harness must assert vocab size 262,144 and the id of <end_of_turn> = 106.
   Measured in this build (tests/tables.md): (a) the new rows are NOT neutral under the ORIGINAL tokenizer: in the control checkpoints (rows re-initialised, original tokenizer)
   the loss on amh rises from 1.35 to 1.73 nats/char (geez N=3000) because the mean-initialised rows of Ethiopic-looking tokens take probability mass that the original
   tokenizer never uses; the swapped model's amh loss is 2.54 nats/char. The C-emb control arm is therefore required AND its first-step loss must be reported next to
   the swap arm's; (b) Latin text: tokenisation, restricted-softmax loss and greedy outputs are identical, the full-softmax loss differs by +0.0002..+0.012 nats/char.
   Known limits of this build (not decided here): new-token rows are initialised, not trained; the SFT-final checkpoint is stored fp32 and the swapped
   checkpoints are bf16 (a --dtype fp32 option exists); llama.cpp / vLLM conversions of the swapped tokenizer are tested only as stated in the build report;
   Fon training text is small (8.9 M characters: Wikipedia fon, mt560 target side, FineWeb-2 fon) and was NOT tuned on FLORES; Ge'ez text is web (jw.org-style heavy) + Wikipedia.
