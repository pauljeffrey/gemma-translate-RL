## 2. Component A: the RL best-translation archive (`gemma_mt/scripts/mix2_archive.py`)

Source: `/workspace/sft_build/runs/rl2c/archive/archive.sqlite` only (6,142 rows, 71 languages; **no rl2 / rl2b archives exist on the box**; the 2c archive covers steps 1..1433; prompt bins b64 385 / b256 3,535 / b512 1,957 / b1024 253 / **b2048 12**). Pipeline (counts per language in `archive_stats_*.json`): `archive_publish.row_problem` (copy / repetition / length ratio: 515 rows) -> word 3/4/5-gram repetition above max(human p97 of the length bucket, the source's own share) (190) -> GlotLID guard of the calibration, diacritic deficit 0, length ratio inside the human window -> thresholds raised above the entry values -> **re-judged by the current QE ensemble** (AfriCOMET-QE + SSA-COMET-MTL, D-55) on the aligned sentences: weighted mean >= 0.9 and 25th percentile >= 0.2 (strict run: 1.0 / 0.3) -> hold-out (exact + 8-gram, the SAME object as mix_v1: 36,817 sentences, 684,273 8-grams; 3 rows hit) -> licence join -> per-language cap 60 k tokens (binds for ibo, lua, twi, yor).
* **Two settings were built.** Strict (qe reward >= 1.4, blend >= 1.25 and consensus >= 60, consensus-only chrF++ >= 66): 1,589 rows, 0.73 M tokens, 53 languages. **Chosen (loose; the coordinator asked for a stronger self-distilled share): qe >= 1.25, blend >= 1.15 and consensus >= 55, consensus-only >= 65, ensemble 0.9 / 0.2: 2,158 rows, 1.04 M tokens, 65 languages** (qe 1,755, blend 259, consensus-only 144; b64 186 / b256 1,309 / b512 623 / **b1024 40 / b2048 0**). 3,984 of 6,142 rows fall out; the biggest reasons: reward below the raised threshold (2,403), repetition (695), ensemble disagreement (307).
* **Consensus-only rows** (23 languages without QE): "excluded unless very high" -> kept at group chrF++ >= 65 (entry bar 58.8, median of eligible 62.8, p90 70.1). Effect: at >= 70 only 54 rows / 9 languages survive, at >= 66 123 rows, at >= 65 144 rows in 17 languages (wes 67, ibb 25, seh 14, nbl 8, ach 5 ...); excluding them completely would leave the 23 languages with 0 archive rows. Caveat: consensus is agreement among 16 T = 1.0 samples, i.e. confidence, not accuracy; the rows are 6.7 % of the archive and < 0.3 % of any mixture.
* The archive is short: median source 686 characters; the long-example count (b1024 40, b2048 0) is why the long-document component exists.

## 3. Component B: long-document distillation (`mix2_select_longdoc.py`, `mix2_gen_vllm.py`, `mix2_longdoc_score.py`, `mix2_longdoc_select.py`)

R6 found whole-document greedy loops / truncates beyond ~512 tokens and sentence-by-sentence + repair gives 55-60 chrF++ at b1024-b4096. **Recipe used (R6's, extended):** documents from the RL pools (`rl_prompts_v3` b512 / b1024, `zz_b2048`) and A4's science pool (`rl_prompts_sci_v1`, pubmed / arxiv / libretexts / arxiv-abstracts, `dsfsi/afriscience_mt` excluded: its human references are already in the science part); 8,778 documents (round 1: 3,696 = 22 b512 + 16 b1024 + 10 b2048 per language; round 2: 5,082 = 26 + 24 + 16, disjoint), 220 k unique sentence requests, hold-out filtered (42 documents hit an 8-gram, 2 a sentence), English prompt <= 1,500 Gemma tokens for b2048 so that the pair fits 8,192. Every sentence is decoded by `final_1433` (vLLM, <= 6 GB) **greedy + 3 samples at T = 0.7** (R6's repair candidates, here for every sentence, about 50 M generated tokens). A candidate must be finished (end-of-turn), have the language tag, be no copy, plausible length, no repetition (anchors thresholds, sentence level), GlotLID-accepted. **Pick per sentence: highest QE-ensemble reward** (54 QE languages; ties -> greedy) or, for the 23 consensus-only languages, the medoid by chrF++ against the other candidates (MBR). Target = the picked sentences joined by single spaces; prompt = the original window exactly as in the RL prompts; the sample ends with `<end_of_turn>` (the model learns to STOP at 500-2,000 words).
* **Best-of-4 is not self-distillation of the greedy output**: on the 2,138 QE documents of round 1 the median document mean reward is 0.82 for the picks against 0.43 for greedy-only (p25 of sentences 0.38 vs 0.01); 65 % of the picked sentences are sampled candidates. 21 % of the documents have a sentence without any valid candidate and are dropped ("all sentences covered").
* **Thresholds are per language and relative** (an absolute "mean reward >= 1.0" passes 0 of 48 amh and 0 of 47 tir documents and would leave 12 languages empty): threshold = the 70th percentile of the language's own documents that pass the hard filters (loop, repetition, whole-text GlotLID, diacritic deficit 0, length window), clipped to [0.3, 1.0] for the mean shaped reward (1.0 = median human reference, 0 = the language's "mismatched" anchor) and [0.0, 0.4] for the 25th percentile; consensus-only: mean agreement in [50, 58.8] chrF++, p25 in [38, 45]. Result (`longdoc_selection_stats.json`): **1,715 documents, 2.83 M tokens (1.87 M target), 71 languages** (b512 857 / b1024 572 / b2048 286; general 1,254 / science 461; QE 1,436 / consensus 279); empty: bbj efi kbp nus shi sid (the model's own output is not certifiable there). Per-language budget 60 k tokens (amh, tir 90 k): amh 37 k, **tir 68 k**. The pool, not the budget, binds (about 25 % of documents pass).
* GPU: 118 k + 80 k + 21 k requests, 3 vLLM runs (0.05-0.07 of the card each, 4-13 k tokens/s on the shared card), QE 0.5 M pairs x 2 models (batch 48-96, < 6 GB): about 2.5 GPU hours in total, spread over three restarts (a process was killed once; nothing was lost, the jobs resume).

## 5. The weak-language regression and how the variants address it
