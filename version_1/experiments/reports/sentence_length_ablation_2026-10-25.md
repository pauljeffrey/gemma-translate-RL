# Sentence-length ablation (how many sentences per translation pass), saved 2026-10-25

Model: final2-step401 on vLLM (RTX 3060). Rule (`translate_pipeline/length_ablation/DECISION_RULE.txt`, written before running): choose the largest k whose chrF++ loss against k = 1 is <= 0.3 (point estimate) and whose coverage (share of source sentences not omitted) is >= 0.98. Results: `translate_pipeline/length_ablation/results/{ref,real}/` (DECISION.md, csv/).
Data: FLORES+ devtest document-ordered excerpts (10 consecutive sentences per excerpt; ALL devtest rows, not only pair-unseen rows: contamination affects every k equally) in swh, hau, yor, kin, amh, tir, built directly from the repo files because the pipeline's reader could not see the devtest split; 150 real Wikipedia-simple passages (no references; the k = 1 output plays the reference, so only omission and failure rate are meaningful there). NTREX not run.

FLORES+ with references (macro over six languages, repair ON = production): chrF++ k=1 40.13; k=2 40.22 (loss -0.08 [-0.26, +0.10]); k=3 40.20 (loss -0.07 [-0.27, +0.15], coverage 0.9935); k=5 39.75 (loss +0.385 [0.083, 0.711]: FAIL); whole excerpt 39.76 (loss +0.373: FAIL). Rule on FLORES+: k=3 chosen (k=1 recommended for amh; with repair OFF also for hau).
Real passages (no references): coverage against k=1 outputs 0.734 (k=2), 0.748 (k=3), 0.680 (k=5), 0.642 (whole passage); rule: only k=1 passes (all larger k fail the coverage and agreement gates). Failure rate (after repair) 0.3% at k=1.
DECISION: keep k = 1 (sentence by sentence) for production. The FLORES+ result alone would allow k = 3, but on real passages (lists, tables, short stub articles) multi-sentence passes diverge from sentence-level output and omit about a quarter of the content, which is the data we translate. Not measured: throughput gain of k = 3 (the CSVs do not include a throughput file in this run), SSA-COMET-QE / AfriCOMET-QE (not installed), NTREX.
