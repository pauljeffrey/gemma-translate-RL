Owner/coordinator changes applied during the run: two domain labels were added to the fetcher (`government_public`, `opinion_magazine`); after the coordinator message of ~15:50 UTC (no medicine, no law in the core) I stopped legislation.gov.uk, PMC, BillSum, court opinions, EUR-Lex, Federal Register, CDC/NIH and did not extend them. What was already written is kept, labelled (`law_government` / `health`), and is excluded from section (c).

Thin domains, honestly:
* **stories**: only Project Gutenberg (289 documents, authors died <= 1954). Nothing modern under a clean licence was found at >= 400 tokens. At a 10% Gutenberg cap the whole stories stratum cannot be filled with trusted non-archaic fiction. Options: label some `opinion_magazine`/narrative features (Minority Africa, Balkan Diskurs, Global Voices, NPS oral-history style) as "narrative", or raise the cap for stories only, or commission/obtain a CC BY fiction anthology (not found).
* **travel/culture**: 231 documents without Gutenberg, of which 210 are GOV.UK FCDO travel advice (OGL) and 10 Pressbooks tourism textbook chapters; no modern travel writing under a clean licence (Wikivoyage is the share-alike split). Roughly 63 documents is feasible, but all from two sources.
* **encyclopedia/explanatory**: without EB1911 only NPS pages (240), Pressbooks/DOAB humanities text (scholarly) are left; the real modern encyclopedia is Wikipedia (share-alike split).
* **news** has plenty of 400-1,600-token capacity but only 160 documents >= 3,200 tokens and 822 >= 1,600; the long ones are Alt News/Factly/Balkan Diskurs/360info features.
* **opinion_magazine**: 351 documents (Freedom of the Press, 360info, New Canadian Media) plus the 1,299 Global Voices posts if you decide to label them that way (they are `news` in the pool file); no Africa-based CC BY magazine was reachable (see (b)).
* **education** and **science_explainer** at 4096: 162 and 131 documents, from DOAB (scholarly) and textbook pages; the 2048 bin is fine.
* Quality note: I read about 5 random documents per new source in full-text previews (not 20), and ran an automatic scan of every new file for URLs, backtick quotes, bracket markup, "Figure/Table N" references and over-long paragraphs (`qc.py`): clean except the "Figure/Table N" references (GovReport 798 of 1,500 documents, Pressbooks 101, LibreTexts 59) and 460 documents of the old `cp_usgpo_a` with `` quotes (cosmetic, from before a fix).

## (d) Reproduce

```
cd /workspace/sft_build/longdoc_bench && . /workspace/sft_build/env.sh >/dev/null 2>&1
export PYTHONPATH=$PWD/code:/workspace/sft_build/pylibs HF_HOME=/workspace/sft_build/hf CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=; PY=/workspace/venv_gemma/bin/python
S="nice -n 10 $PY code/scripts/longdoc_fetch_sources.py"
$S govreport  --out pool/govreport__pd.jsonl                 --max-docs 1500          # 130 s
$S cp_news    --out pool/cp_news__permissive.jsonl           --max-docs 1800          # 50 s, streamed
$S cp_books   --out pool/cp_pressbooks__permissive.jsonl     --max-docs 1200 --only pressbooks --per-book 3
$S cp_books   --out pool/cp_libretexts__permissive.jsonl     --max-docs 600  --only libretexts --per-book 3
```

Streaming from the Hub needs the read-only token (`env.sh`) only for rate limits; nothing is written to the Hub. Politeness: only `legislation`, `pmc` and `eb1911` (gutenberg.org) touched web servers (User-Agent with contact, 5 s / 1 s delays); all other new sources read Hub datasets. Earlier versions of regenerated files are in `tmp/old/` on the rental. Dedup: Pool now also drops exact repeated paragraphs inside one document (affects only new runs).

## (e) Could not reach / recommendations

* CRS (crsreports.congress.gov, congress.gov) 403, Africa Check 403, EUR-Lex 202 (WAF), World Bank OKR API disallowed by robots.txt, travel.gc.ca not openly licensed, un.org not redistributable.
* Ask the World Bank (OKR) for a bulk export of CC BY 3.0 IGO / 4.0 Africa reports (text bitstreams exist) and the WMT organisers about redistribution if WMT news is wanted.
* Keep GovReport but label the CRS half as "statement not verified", or drop it; GAO terms are clear. Both halves are a public benchmark and will be flagged by the contamination scan.
* For stories and travel the clean-licence supply is thin; the practical choices are a Gutenberg cap exception for stories, narrative features as "stories", and Wikivoyage as the labelled share-alike travel split.
* The Common Pile is the right "trusted" umbrella (paper, per-document licence strings); its own warning about licence laundering should be quoted in the release notes, and spot checks of licence strings (done for the news outlets' own pages: Africa Is a Country CC BY 4.0, SciDev.Net CC BY) are advisable before release.
