## Title (chosen and why)
"Small but Sabi: How Far Can Reinforcement Learning Take a 270M Translator into 77 African Languages?". *Sabi* (Nigerian Pidgin, "to know / to know how") plays on "small but mighty" and signals the African-language focus and the project name; the subtitle states the method (RL with a reward that is defended against gaming), the model size and the coverage, all of which the paper measures. Not claimed: "state of the art", "human preferences", or that RL replaces SFT. Alternatives if the venue prefers a plainer title: "Reinforcement Learning with a Hardened Quality-Estimation Reward for English-to-African Translation with a 270M Model".
