\section{Related work}
% Long paper: Section 2 (compressed)
\paragraph{African MT and evaluation.} Data and benchmarks come from community efforts: Masakhane \citep{nekoto2020participatory}, MAFAND-MT \citep{adelani2022mafand}, FLORES-200 and NLLB-200 \citep{goyal2022flores101,nllb2022nllb}, FLORES+ \citep{oldi2025floresplus}, MADLAD-400 \citep{kudugunta2023madlad}, SALT \citep{akera2022salt} and SMOL \citep{caswell2025smol}. Quality estimation for African languages is led by AfriCOMET \citep{wang2024africomet} and SSA-COMET \citep{li2025ssacomet}, with human annotations for only a handful of pairs. Larger LLM-based work adapts 4--14B models \citep{yu2026afriquellm,akera2025sunflower}; TranslatePsy-AfriSLM is the nearest small competitor, 0.8--4B models for 19 languages trained by supervised fine-tuning with QE filtering \citep{gritta2026translatepsy}. NLLB-200's model card states that inputs were limited to 512 tokens and that it is not intended for document translation \citep{nllb200_card}; African document-level benchmarks exist \citep{alabi2025afridocmt,abdulmumin2026afriscience}.
\paragraph{RL for translation.} GRPO \citep{shao2024deepseekmath} underlies R1-style translation work with 3--7B and larger models \citep{feng2025mtr1zero,cheng2025seedx,zheng2025hunyuanmt}; TranslateGemma uses a reward-model ensemble for Gemma~3 at 4B and above \citep{finkelstein2026translategemma}. Closest to our setting are RL on NLLB-600M/1.3B with a round-trip chrF++ reward \citep{attia2026roundtrip}, reference-free GRPO on NLLB with COMET-Kiwi-style rewards \citep{garciaestrada2026reffree} and RL for unseen-language translation \citep{hu2026rlunseen}. Optimising a learned reward over-optimises it \citep{gao2023scaling,krakovna2020specification}, ensembles mitigate this \citep{coste2024ensembles}, and for translation WALAR and MiLMMT-style work document hacks such as non-translations and wrong-language output and gate the reward by language identification \citep{liu2026walar,han2026milmmtrl}, a job done for low-resource languages by GlotLID \citep{kargaran2023glotlid}. Whether RL generalises where SFT memorises \citep{chu2025sft} or only sharpens \citep{yue2025doesrl} is open for reasoning, and we know of no controlled comparison for translation.
\paragraph{Positioning.} We claim no priority for RL in MT, for small models, for reward-hacking analysis or for language coverage: NLLB-200 supports 55 African languages and MADLAD-400 lists about 85 \citep{nllb2022nllb,kudugunta2023madlad} (our tally, unverified). What we add is a measured account, for a 270M model and 77 African targets, of what a reward needs around it, and a controlled small-scale test of RL against continued SFT.
