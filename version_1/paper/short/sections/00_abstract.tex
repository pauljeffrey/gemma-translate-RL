\noindent A 270M-parameter model can write 77 African languages, but fine-tuning alone leaves it with sentences it never finishes, source text it copies and languages it barely writes. We ask how far reinforcement learning (GRPO) with quality-estimation rewards can close that gap without any new parallel data. On FLORES+, RL lifts macro chrF++ from 28.7 to 34.1 (+5.4, 95\% CI $[5.2, 5.6]$; all 45 languages improve), most where the fine-tuned model was weakest (Kabuverdianu +14.2, Kabiye +14.1); part of that gain is repaired unfinished or looping output. \emph{The test sets were not unseen by our pipeline}: the SFT text held the English side of 95\% of FLORES+ devtest, the eight benchmarks are five English texts, and the reward was calibrated on some test splits (Efik thresholds on IbomMT; the final run's anchors on six sets), so our levels are inflated (by 3.8 chrF++ for SFT and 2.6 for RL on FLORES+) and some results are not independent tests; on rows never seen in training RL still beats SFT on all eight benchmarks (FLORES+ +6.6, NTREX-128 +4.1, MAFAND-MT +3.7, SmolSent +7.1, SALT +2.3), and a fresh evaluation is planned, not done. A pre-registered ablation (four languages, one run per arm) shows that the gain survives a much weaker fine-tuning start (+7.4 against +5.7 chrF++) and that RL beats continued fine-tuning of equal compute (+7.0); it does not show that RL replaces fine-tuning. On identical sentences the model stays 3.32 chrF++ behind NLLB-200 3.3B on the 42 languages without the two Ge'ez-script languages (median 2.78; 3.85 and 3.17 on all 44, because a few languages carry the gap: Kabuverdianu, Amharic and Tigrinya 30\%); Amharic and Tigrinya, left out as an outlier with an untested cause, are 19.0 and 11.0 behind, and Kabuverdianu, our largest gain over SFT, is also the largest gap (21.0); we do not beat NLLB. Quantised to 8 bits it runs on four CPU cores with no measurable quality loss (about two sentences per second on a server CPU). We evaluate against human references, metrics that correlate with human judgement (AfriCOMET, SSA-COMET) and an independent LLM judge (Spearman 0.5 with human ratings); there is no human evaluation, and we say where our reward and our metrics overlap.
