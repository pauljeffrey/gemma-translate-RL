## Optimisation
* KL (k3, coefficient 0.05): median 0.034 over steps 1-50 -> 0.149 over steps 351-400; median over the run 0.137, max 0.169. The guard (threshold 0.15 on a 5-step median) cut the learning rate at steps 226 (x0.5), 236 (x0.25) and 247 (x0.125); it never recovered, so steps 247-400 ran at 2.5e-7 instead of 2e-6 (record the effective learning rate with the result; the dev curve was flat before the first cut, so the cuts did not cause the plateau).
* **Why the KL rose.** KL median per 10 steps: 0.028 (steps 1-10), 0.034 (21-30), 0.043 (31-40) before the features, then 0.057 (41-50), 0.074, 0.089, 0.107, 0.122 (81-90), and flat at 0.124-0.129 from step ~90 to 160, 0.149 by the end. The slope tripled when the features started at step 41 (0.00053 per step over steps 2-40, 0.00143 over steps 41-100). The logs cannot separate the four features because they started together and no per-row-type KL is logged. What the metrics do say: the advantage std floor never binds (group-std p10 0.128 vs floor 0.02) and dynamic sampling never acted (flat fraction 0, no refill round), so neither can explain it; what remains is the **beam guide** (its tokens are in the k3 KL term; in 25% of the groups it is a member with a mean advantage of +0.85 and a reward of -0.19 vs -0.61 for the sampled members during steps 41-100, so it pulls the policy consistently toward beam-like output) and the **reference domain** (15% of the prompts with a chrF++ term of weight 0.35 against human references; mean shift of the reward -0.21). The guide is the more likely driver, but this is an inference, not a measurement. B1 keeps the guide and drops the reference domain, so B1's KL versus stage A's is a weak test of the reference domain (same guide, no reference).
* **Owner decisions applied to B1 and mixed (2026-11-03):** `kl_guard_threshold` 0.15 -> 0.25 (cut/recover logic otherwise unchanged), `dynamic_sampling_oversample` 1.1 -> 1.0 (refill logic and cap 2 kept: no group was flat in stage A, so the 10% surplus was waste), beam guide kept (guide_fraction 0.25), reference domain OFF in B1. ## Why stage A ran its full 400-step cap (early stopping)
Dev chrF++ was flat from step ~100, but the stage ran to the cap: `plateau_stop` was "not evaluated" (guard_event / lr_low) from step ~226 on, because the KL guard had cut the learning rate, and the training-reward `early_stop` (three series: smoothed training reward, finished rate, greedy loop rate; any new best resets the counter) kept seeing small new bests (reward at steps 251 and 345, loop rate at 287, finished rate at 214); the longest stale stretch was 57 steps, below the stage-A patience of 150 and the B1 patience of 80. The new dev-based early stop (`early_stop_dev`, patch 2) watches the dev chrF++ macro and weak_dev directly (k = 3 dev evaluations every 25 steps, eps 0.15, min 75 steps, finished rate >= 0.98) and has no learning-rate condition; replayed on the real stage-A series at the stage-A cadence of 50 steps it fires only at step 400, at 25 steps it fires about 75 steps after the last gain.
