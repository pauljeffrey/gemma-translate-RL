## 3. Method facts checked against the files (M1, M4, M7, M9)

* **M1 (architecture).** `research_archive_2026-11-03/runs_385m/A/best/config.json` (copy: `reports/data/e23_hygiene_2026-11-04/model_config_stageA_best.json`): Gemma3ForCausalLM, hidden 640, 39 layers, 4 attention heads, 1 KV head, head_dim 256, intermediate 2048, vocab 262,144, sliding window 512, 3 full-attention layers (sliding_window_pattern 13), max positions 32,768. The parameter count recomputed from this config is exactly 385,144,448 (`build_sft_curve.py`), as stated in M1.
* **M4 (SFT recipe and curve).** Run records of stage 1 (g1a, 1,500 steps) and stage 2 (g1b_s1..s6) copied into `sft_385m_curve.csv` (3,423 logged rows, 137 validation points). Final: step 32,853, validation loss macro 1.3412, micro 1.2846 over 78 languages (`sft_385m_val_by_language_final.csv`); end of stage 1 macro 1.7847. Optimiser Muon (momentum 0.95, 5 Newton-Schulz steps; AdamW on embeddings and norms), weight decay 0.01, beta (0.9, 0.95), grad clip 1.0, 32 packs x 4,096 = 131,072 tokens per step, 3% linear warm-up, cosine to 10%; peak LR 5e-5 for stage 1 and shard 1, 3e-5 for shards 2-6 (final LR of the last step 3.0e-6); seed 1234 (`sft_configs/*.yaml`, `summary_*.json`). Shard step ranges: g1a 1-1500, s1 1510-6326, s2 6330-11631, s3 11640-16936, s4 16940-22242, s5 22250-27548, s6 27550-32853. The macro validation loss rises from 1.78 (end of stage 1) to 1.98 at the end of shard 1 because new languages enter the validation set at stage 2 (shard-by-shard values in the CSV).
* **M7 (RL algorithm).** From `runs_385m/{A,B1,mixed}/config.json` (copies in `rl_configs/`): optimiser Muon (momentum 0.95, 5 NS steps), LR 2e-6 with 10 warm-up steps, weight decay 0, grad clip 1.0, token-level loss aggregation, KL coefficient 0.05 with estimator k3c, KL guard 0.15 (stage A) and 0.25 (B1, mixed), temperature 1.0, top-p 1.0, group 16, prompts per step 64 / 48 / 42 (stage A / B1 / mixed), max new tokens 384 for b64 and 1280 for b256 and mixed, seed 1234, selection by dev chrF++ with a minimum gain of 0.1. The optimiser gap named in M7 is closed: Muon, not AdamW.
* **M9 (reward).** `gemma_mt/gemma_mt/rl/qe_ensemble.py` header: the QE part is r = (r1 + r2) / 2 when |r1 - r2| <= delta, else min(r1, r2), r1 = shaped AfriCOMET-QE, r2 = shaped SSA-COMET-MTL reference-free; delta = 1.0 reward units (`ens_delta` in `clean_rerun_misc/assets/calibration/shaping_clean_v2.json`); 42 `qe2` languages (41 with the ensemble block; dyu has no ensemble block); reward range [-2, 2.25] (training_state `reward_range`); consensus on with at least 3 valid members and blend beta 0.5; GlotLID `model_v3` as the language guard with a 15-character minimum; NLLB pseudo-reference signal for kea kbp nus bam kab with weight 0.25 (max 0.35); reference weight 0.35; `longdoc_v2` chunked reward (sentence chunking) on; end-of-turn discipline, word-ratio and length penalties on.
