# Post-B1 evaluation of the 385M model (2026-11-03)

Systems (all greedy, vLLM, max 768 new tokens, same prompt and harness as `reports/eval_385m_results_2026-11-03.md`; English to X):
* **385M SFT** = `g15_385m_sft_final` (the earlier evaluation, re-used, not re-run);
* **stage A** = RL stage A final (step 400, Hub `<HF_ORG>/gemma-0.4B-translate-rl@stage-a-final`);
* **B1 best** = RL stage B1 best by dev chrF++ (step 50, dev 40.743; Hub `@stage-b1-b256`). B1 ended on a dev plateau at step 135 (dev 40.74 / 40.74 / 40.72 / 40.48 at 50 / 75 / 100 / 125); its final model (dev 40.56) is on the Hub as `@stage-b1-final` and was not evaluated;
* **old 270M SFT / RL** = the first research version (SFT-final step 27,638, RL-final GRPO step 1,433), taken from the stored harness runs (`reports/data/eval_385m_plan_2026-11-02/own_270m_per_language.csv`), same decoding and metrics, not re-run.
Metrics: chrF++ and spBLEU (flores200 tokenizer), finished rate; reference-based AfriCOMET with the held-out `masakhane/africomet-stl-1.1` (not a reward model; 41 FLORES+ languages it covers); 95% paired sentence-bootstrap intervals (1,000 resamples, equal language weight) for the three 385M systems. The mixed stage has not produced a checkpoint yet; its comparison is added when it has dev evaluations. Data, scripts and CSVs: `reports/data/eval_post_B1_2026-11-03/` (`build_report_tables.py` regenerates the tables below).

## Summary
* RL is worth +8 chrF++ on FLORES+ devtest over the SFT model (22.85 -> 30.81 after stage A, -> 31.35 for B1 best; 54 languages), +5 spBLEU, +0.07 held-out AfriCOMET (0.554 -> 0.623 -> 0.625). Finished rate 0.83 -> 0.98 -> 0.99. Every one of the 54 languages improves with B1 best over SFT (95% CI excludes 0 in all 54); the weak list gains slightly more (+8.9) than the other languages (+8.3).
* B1 adds little over stage A: +0.54 chrF++ on FLORES+ (CI +0.49 to +0.59; better in 32 of 54 languages, no clear difference in 22, worse in none), +0.3 on NTREX (36.68 -> 36.98), 0.0 on MAFAND, +0.2 on AfriScience (within-model series only); dev chrF++ moved 40.50 -> 40.74 only. Stage B1 (b256) cost 4.2 h for this.
* Against the first research version (old 270M): on NTREX the 385M RL models are ahead of the old RL on the clean rows (36.7-37.0 vs 35.6); on MAFAND they are 1.0 below the old RL on the clean rows (40.8 vs 41.8); FLORES+ is marked: the old models saw it in training (95% of the devtest English), so no verdict rests on it (385M B1 best 34.6 vs old RL 31.7 on the 45 clean-row languages, 34.3 on all rows). AfriScience is **not comparable until the 385M has had its science SFT/RL stage** (the old 270M had one), so it is excluded from every verdict. The 385M SFT alone is below the old SFT on NTREX/MAFAND (see the earlier report). **Basis for the keep-RL decision: NTREX and MAFAND, clean rows only**: RL takes the 385M from below the old SFT to above the old RL on NTREX (36.7-37.0 vs 35.6) and to within 1.0 of the old RL on MAFAND (40.8 vs 41.8, still behind).

## Caveats
* The old models saw the FLORES+ devtest English (95% of rows) in SFT and parts of NTREX / MAFAND / SmolSent; the `clean` rows are the ones whose English and pair were not in its SFT text (NTREX clean 1,201 rows per language on average, MAFAND 1,042, FLORES+ only 48). Lead with NTREX clean and MAFAND clean; FLORES is marked (contaminated for the old model). AfriScience is not used in the old-vs-385M comparison (the 385M has had no science stage yet); its numbers below are a within-model series.
* The 385M systems are the clean rerun (protected index, no benchmark text in SFT or RL). The contamination audit of the NEW SFT text (stage-B additions) is still pending, so 385M columns are all rows, labelled unaudited. RL prompts: the 7 new-language prompt files contained flagged English windows (about 0.15% of those languages' prompts; source side only), see `reports/rl_stage_A_385m_2026-11-03.md`.
* Old per-language numbers come from earlier runs of the same harness, but a different code state; no bootstrap intervals exist for them. One training seed per model. B1 best was chosen by dev chrF++ (720 dev sentences, 16 languages), not on any of the benchmarks above.
* Languages that the held-out AfriCOMET does not cover have no AfriCOMET entry ('-'); the weak group is the 20 FLORES+ languages of the x2.5 RL weight list.
