## 0. Pre-registration (written 2026-11-05 05:20 UTC; no E-04 step has run)

**Question.** The stage-A RL gain over the SFT (+3.7 dev chrF++ in 400 steps, +3.3 of it in the first 100) is one run with one seed. How large is the seed-to-seed spread of the gain at step 100? This is the noise yardstick for every small RL difference in the paper (B1 +0.54, mixed +0.31, science RL) and for the E-11 ablations.

**Runs.** Two new runs, seeds **20261105** (tag s2) and **20261106** (tag s3); the executed stage A used seed 1234 (seed 1). Everything else is the executed stage A:
* Start = SFT final `<HF_ORG>/gemma-0.4B-translate-sft` (= KL reference), fresh Muon optimiser, lr 2e-6, warm-up 10, KL coefficient 0.05, KL guard threshold 0.15, 64 prompts x 16 samples (15 sampled + 1 greedy), b64 bin, max 384 new tokens, same reward (africomet-qe-stl-1.1 + ssa-comet-mtl ensemble, calibration `shaping_clean_v2.json`, GlotLID language guard, consensus utility, length/omission/anchor penalties, NLLB-3.3B pseudo-reference signal for 5 languages kea kbp nus bam kab, reference-anchored chrF++ term for amh efi hau lug nso yor zul), same prompt pool `clean_v3_385m` (81 languages, the 7 unfiltered new-language files have since been filtered: 6 of 28,120 drawn prompts were flagged in seed 1; the flagged prompts are not in the pool any more), same language weights (weak list x2.5) and domain weights.
* **Schedule as executed:** stage A ran steps 1-40 WITHOUT the four features (dynamic sampling, advantage std floor 0.02, beam-guided group member, reference domain) and steps 41-400 WITH them (stopped at the step-40 checkpoint and resumed with the new config). The seeds do the same: base config to the step-40 checkpoint (written by the trainer), SIGTERM, config swapped, the supervisor restarts the trainer from `last/` (steps 41-100 with the features). The config of steps 41+ equals the executed `runs_385m/A/config.json` except: seed, steps 100, `hub_*` null (nothing is pushed), the translation archive off, dev predictions saved, extension/plateau/early stops off (none can act within 100 steps). `e_cfgcheck.py` prints these differences against the executed config.
* **What changes between seeds:** the trainer seed only (prompt schedule = function of seed and step, vLLM sampling seed, trainer RNG). Hardware, code state (hashes in `code_state.txt` of each run), pool, reward models and configs are identical.
* **Pre-declared step: 100 (the final model).** The trainer also keeps a `best/` by dev chrF++ at its evaluations (steps 0, 50, 100); it is reported separately as "best by dev" and never replaces the step-100 numbers.
* Hardware/engine: A100 80 GB, rollouts and evaluation with vLLM 0.32.0 bf16; nothing else runs on the GPU during a seed run.

**Data used by every seed run (disclosure).** Prompts: FineWeb-Edu and Wikipedia English windows (general), BBC News / Wikinews / Global Voices / Wikivoyage English (news, 25.5% of the draw from step 41, 30% before), reference domain (15% from step 41: AfriScience-MT train, Apache-2.0, and IbomMT train, CC-BY-NC-4.0, human translations). Reward side: africomet-qe-stl-1.1, ssa-comet-mtl, GlotLID v3, NLLB-3.3B pseudo-references (CC-BY-NC, reward only, 5 languages). No TranslatePsy, no NLLB-mined training text, no NLLB model output as a training target. Full list: `paper_385m/DISCLOSURES.md` section 1.2.

**Evaluation (fixed now).**
1. *In-training dev* (exactly seed 1's evaluation): 720 FLORES+ dev held-out-half sentences (16 languages), greedy, 256 new tokens, at steps 0, 50, 100: chrF++ macro, BLEU, finished rate, weak_dev (20 weak languages). Seed 1 values at step 100 (STA): dev chrF++ 40.14, BLEU 14.46, finished 0.988, weak_dev 26.02 (step 0: 36.81 / 12.46 / 0.933 / 20.76). These are macro numbers; seed 1's dev predictions were not saved, so no sentence-level interval against seed 1 exists. Evaluation noise of the dev macro: sd 0.09-0.10 chrF++ (STA).
2. *Benchmarks, 20 languages* (yor hau ibo swh amh zul xho kin som wol lug sna twi ewe lin tir orm nso tsn afr): the standard harness on the step-100 model: FLORES+ devtest (all 1,012 sentences), NTREX, MAFAND (those of the 20 languages that have them), vLLM 0.32.0 bf16, greedy, 768 new tokens, no decode-time guard, held-out AfriCOMET-STL-1.1. Compared on identical sentences with the SFT, stage A final (400 steps), B1 best and mixed best (stored predictions of the same harness; 214 contamination-flagged rows removed).
3. Loops/degeneration with both detectors (reward-family flag and the independent detector of E-08), finished rate, plain chrF / chrF++ and held-out AfriCOMET next to anything from the reward family (the reward-family QE scores are never shown alone; SSA-COMET-QE is not computed here).

**Quantities and decision rules.**
* Gain of a seed g_s = (seed s at step 100) - SFT, chrF++ macro over the 20 languages (FLORES+ / NTREX / MAFAND and all cells), with paired-bootstrap interval (Poisson weights per source sentence, 1,000 resamples, seed 20261104).
* **Seed spread** = difference between the two new seeds on identical sentences (s2 - s3, chrF++ and AfriCOMET, with intervals), and on the in-training dev the range of the three seeds' macros (seed 1, s2, s3). With only two (devtest) or three (dev) runs the spread is a crude estimate; it is reported as "observed spread", not as a standard deviation. The E-11 rule uses it: an ablation effect is called real only if its paired interval excludes 0 and its point difference exceeds the observed seed difference |s2 - s3| on the same metric.
* The seeds "reproduce" stage A's gain if both seeds' dev chrF++ at step 100 are within the range [seed 1 - 0.3, seed 1 + 0.3] = [39.84, 40.44]; larger deviations are reported as a spread of that size.
* Null/negative results are reported as such: if the seeds differ by more than the B1 (+0.54) or mixed (+0.31) steps, those steps are "not separable from seed noise".

**Not done:** a second SFT seed (stage B alone is 44.8 h), seeds of later stages, more than two extra seeds, evaluation of seed 1 at step 100 on the benchmarks (its step-100 weights were never saved; only its dev metrics exist).

## 2. Verdict

* **The stage-A gain is reproducible across training seeds at step 100.** Gains over the SFT: +5.96 [+5.80, +6.11] and +5.90 [+5.74, +6.05] chrF++ (all 49 cells), +0.046 and +0.045 held-out AfriCOMET; the two seeds differ by **0.06 chrF++ [-0.02, +0.14]** and by 0.0008 AfriCOMET: the observed seed-to-seed difference is about 1% of the gain and at the level of the evaluation noise. Per language the seeds differ by up to 0.5 chrF++ (som, zul significant), so per-language differences under about 0.5 chrF++ are not separable from training noise. Dev-set gains of the three runs (seed 1, s2, s3) are +3.33, +3.70, +3.46.
* **What this means for earlier claims.** The B1 step (+0.17 [+0.12, +0.21] over stage A on these 20 languages; the 54-language figure of the paper is +0.54 in the dev metric) is about three times the observed seed difference (0.06) and its interval excludes 0; the mixed step over B1 (+0.07 [+0.02, +0.11]) is the size of the observed seed difference and is **not separable from seed noise**; stage A to mixed (+0.23 [+0.18, +0.29]) is about four times the seed difference. These statements use ONE pair of seeds at step 100; the seed spread of later stages (400+ steps, other recipes) is not measured, so they are indications, not tests.
* **Step 100 is not the end of the benchmark gain:** the 400-step stage A is +0.78 (s2) and +0.84 (s3) chrF++ above the 100-step seeds on the benchmarks (+0.009 AfriCOMET), although the dev macro was flat from step 100; the 100-step seeds still have finished 0.989 and loop flag 0.012-0.013 against 0.998 and 0.005 at step 400. The comparison "seed spread vs ablation effect" in E-11 is therefore made at step 100, as pre-registered.
* The registered "seeds reproduce stage A" criterion (dev gain within +-0.3 of seed 1) holds for s3 and misses for s2 by 0.07.

## 3. Caveats / not done
Two extra seeds only (three with the executed run, but seed 1 has dev metrics only, no step-100 weights); one SFT start (a second SFT seed stays impractical); seed 1's benchmark numbers exist only at step 400. The dev subsets differ between seeds (Amendment 1), so dev-based comparisons across seeds are of gains, not paired sentence sets. Evaluation used 20 languages; the 54-language behaviour of the 100-step seeds was not evaluated. The reward-family QE scores were not used as evidence; the loop flags are shown with both detectors (the reward's flag and the independent one).
